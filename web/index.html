Anatomy of a 13-digit ID

The same rules implemented in src/utils/validator.py.

YYMMDD

Date of birth

Century is omitted. Both 19YY and 20YY are tested and the younger plausible date wins.

SSSS

Gender sequence

0000–4999 is recorded as female, 5000–9999 as male.

C

Citizenship

0 = South African citizen. 1 = permanent resident. Any other digit is rejected.

A

Legacy digit

Historically a race classification. Currently 8 or 9 and unused by modern systems.

Z

Luhn check digit

Modulus-10 checksum over the first twelve digits. Catches transpositions and typos.

120 yrs

Plausibility window

Future dates and ages beyond 120 years are rejected as implausible.
